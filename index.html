Curriculum Vitae — updated 2026

Working across scales, from cell to circuit —
modelling how biological systems hold their shape and change it.

I'm a PhD candidate at McGill University studying the dynamics of biological systems: how populations of cells, neurons, and replicators organize, cooperate, and fail. My work moves between analytical models, numerical simulation, and — increasingly — the tools of computational neuroscience and NeuroAI.
